IV · Implementation

A system is judged by the people who use it every day.

I build and roll out in stages, train the people who will rely on the system, and plan for access, backups and support so it keeps working long after launch.

My role
School onboarding, teacher training, learner adoption, stakeholder engagement and commercial development.

Finance documentationIn development

Wazi

When requests, approvals and receipts move through paper and email, it is hard to show who approved what, when, and on what evidence.

My role
Systems design and development: mapping the finance process, designing the workflow and permissions, and building the system.
Approach
A structured workflow with defined roles, required evidence at each step, and a permanent record of every action.

What I bring is the combination: enough technical depth to design and build systems, and enough organizational awareness to see them adopted, secured and maintained.
