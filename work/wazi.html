Selected work / Case study

Wazi

Digital finance documentation: approval workflows, accountability and evidence management.

Status
In development
Sector
Finance operations
My role
Systems design and development
Image
Illustrative, not a product screenshot

Context

Wazi is a digital systems initiative for finance documentation within an organizational environment, where every approval and supporting document needs to be traceable.

The problem

When requests, approvals and receipts move through paper and email, it is hard to show who approved what, when, and on what evidence.

Approvals depend on individuals being available, supporting documents become separated from the requests they justify, and putting together a record for review takes time.

My role

  • Mapping the finance documentation process and the people involved.
  • Designing the approval workflow, roles and permissions.
  • Structuring how records and supporting evidence are stored.
  • Developing the digital system.

Approach

Each request becomes a structured record with its supporting evidence attached. It moves through defined steps, from request to review, approval and recording, and each step can only be completed by the role responsible for it.

Every action is written to a permanent audit trail with the person and time, and recorded items are locked against later edits. Accountability is designed into the system rather than checked afterwards.

Status and evidence

In development. No results are claimed yet. The measures that will matter are the completeness of records and the time from request to approval.
